FAQ

## Questions Logistics Buyers Ask

Clear answers for companies planning transport, fleet capacity, and supply-chain execution in Saudi Arabia.

### What logistics services does a2b provide in Saudi Arabia?

a2b Logistics provides B2B trucking, road freight, warehousing support, customs-clearance coordination, supply-chain support, cargo insurance, and fleet capacity across Saudi Arabia.

### Does a2b support international companies entering the Saudi market?

Yes. a2b supports international companies, factories, procurement teams, and operations teams that need local logistics infrastructure, fleet access, and Saudi Arabia-wide execution from day one.

### Which areas does a2b cover?

a2b operates across the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, including major cities, domestic road routes, and support for sea and air port logistics.

### What fleet and booking models are available?

a2b offers multiple truck types and sizes, including closed trucks, flatbeds, refrigerated and freezer trucks, boom trucks, cranes, pickups, water tankers, and lowbed trailers. Booking models include per-trip bookings, period contracts, and unit-rate arrangements.

### How quickly can a2b respond to logistics requests?

a2b provides 24/7 operations support and works to identify and allocate the right equipment with minimal lead time based on route, cargo type, timing, and project requirements.
